Delete Your ApplyMate Account

```

ApplyMate users can permanently delete their account and associated ApplyMate data.

Delete your account inside the app

  1. Open ApplyMate.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the Profile screen.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This permanently deletes the ApplyMate account and the server-managed data associated with that account.

If you no longer have access to the app

Email support.applymate@gmail.com with the subject ApplyMate Account Deletion Request.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ApplyMate account where possible.

We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before completing an email-based deletion request.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes backend-owned ApplyMate data including:

  • Your ApplyMate user account
  • Your job applications
  • Your reminders
  • Your active refresh/authentication sessions
  • Outstanding email-verification challenge data
  • Outstanding password-reset challenge data

The app also clears account-specific local state on that device, including authentication information, reminder-notification identifiers and Gmail integration/processing state associated with the deleted ApplyMate account.

Deleting an ApplyMate account does not delete your Google Account or the email messages held by Google. ApplyMate does not store copies of your Gmail mailbox in its backend database.

Once ApplyMate account deletion has completed, the deleted account cannot be recovered.

Questions

Contact support.applymate@gmail.com if you need help with account deletion.
```
