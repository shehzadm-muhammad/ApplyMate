Delete Your ApplyMate Account

ApplyMate users can permanently delete their account and associated data.

Delete your account inside the app

  1. Open ApplyMate.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the Profile screen.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This permanently deletes your ApplyMate account and associated job application and reminder data.

If you no longer have access to the app

Email support.applymate@gmail.com with the subject ApplyMate Account Deletion Request.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ApplyMate account where possible.

We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before completing an email-based deletion request.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes:

  • Your ApplyMate user account
  • Your job applications
  • Your reminders
  • Your active authentication sessions

Once deletion has been completed, the account cannot be recovered.
