ApplyMate Privacy Policy

```

Last updated: 29 August 2026

ApplyMate is a mobile application that helps users organise and track job applications, application progress and reminders.

Information we collect

When you create and use an ApplyMate account, we may process:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your securely hashed account password
  • Job application information that you choose to enter
  • Application statuses, salary information, notes and related job details
  • Reminder information that you create
  • Authentication and session information required to secure your account

How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and maintain your ApplyMate account
  • Verify your email address
  • Authenticate you securely
  • Provide password recovery and account-security notifications
  • Store and display your job applications
  • Provide dashboard statistics
  • Provide reminder functionality
  • Maintain your signed-in session
  • Protect user accounts and isolate data between users

Gmail integration

ApplyMate contains an optional Gmail recruitment-email integration. Public production access to this integration may be disabled while required Google verification is pending.

When Gmail integration is enabled and you explicitly choose to connect a Google account, ApplyMate requests read-only Gmail access using the gmail.readonly permission.

ApplyMate may read Gmail message identifiers, sender information, subject, date, snippets and limited textual message content where needed to identify recruitment-related emails and suggest possible updates to your job applications.

Gmail access is user-initiated. ApplyMate does not silently change an application based on an email. A suggested application update is sent to the normal ApplyMate application API only after you explicitly confirm it.

Google data storage and transfer

  • Gmail API requests are made directly from the mobile device to Google.
  • Google access tokens are not sent to the ApplyMate backend.
  • Gmail message bodies and snippets are not stored in the ApplyMate PostgreSQL database.
  • Raw MIME content and Gmail attachments are not retrieved by the integration.
  • Fetched message text is processed transiently and is not persisted as Gmail message content.
  • Only limited local processing state, such as processed-message identifiers and suggestion metadata, may be stored on your device.
  • Disconnecting Gmail removes ApplyMate's local Gmail connection and processing state.

ApplyMate's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the applicable Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

ApplyMate may request permission to schedule local notifications on your device for reminders you create. Reminder-notification scheduling is handled on your device.

Data storage and security

ApplyMate's backend services use HTTPS/TLS connections. Account passwords are not stored in plain text.

Authentication refresh tokens are stored securely on the mobile device, and only protected/hashed refresh-session information is stored by the backend.

Production ApplyMate application data is stored using managed cloud infrastructure.

Service providers

ApplyMate uses service providers necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Render for backend application hosting
  • Neon for managed PostgreSQL database hosting
  • Resend for transactional account emails
  • Google for optional Google account authorisation and Gmail API access
  • Expo Application Services for mobile application build and distribution infrastructure

Data sharing and advertising

ApplyMate does not sell personal information.

ApplyMate does not currently contain advertising or behavioural advertising SDKs and does not use personal information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Data may be processed by service providers where necessary to deliver ApplyMate functionality, maintain infrastructure, provide transactional email or fulfil a user-requested Google integration.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your ApplyMate account from within the app by opening Profile and selecting Delete Account.

Account deletion removes the ApplyMate user account and associated applications, reminders, active authentication sessions and related backend account data.

The mobile application also clears account-specific local ApplyMate state, including local authentication information, reminder-notification state and Gmail integration state associated with the deleted account.

If you no longer have access to the app, visit our account deletion page.

Contact

For privacy, account-deletion or support questions, contact: support.applymate@gmail.com
```
