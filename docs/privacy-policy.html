ApplyMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

ApplyMate is a mobile application that helps users organise and track their job applications.

Information we collect

When you create an ApplyMate account, we may process:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your encrypted/hashed account password
  • Job application information that you choose to enter
  • Application statuses, notes and related details
  • Reminder information that you create
  • Authentication and session information required to keep your account secure

How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and maintain your ApplyMate account
  • Authenticate you securely
  • Store and display your job applications
  • Provide dashboard statistics
  • Provide reminder functionality
  • Maintain your signed-in session
  • Protect user accounts and isolate data between users

Notifications

ApplyMate may request permission to schedule local notifications on your device for reminders you create. Notification scheduling is handled on your device.

Data storage and security

ApplyMate's backend services use secure HTTPS connections. Account passwords are not stored in plain text.

Authentication refresh tokens are stored securely on the mobile device, and only hashed refresh-token values are stored by the backend.

Production application data is stored using managed cloud infrastructure.

Data sharing

ApplyMate does not sell personal information.

Data may be processed by infrastructure providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as hosting and database providers.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your ApplyMate account from within the app by opening Profile and selecting Delete Account.

Deleting your account removes your ApplyMate account and associated application and reminder data.

If you no longer have access to the app, visit our account deletion page.

Contact
